# SYD Billing & Accounting — Brand Standard

Status: Approved standard for EAZYTAXATION project assets.

## Master icon
- Canvas: 1024 × 1024 px
- Background: Midnight Navy #10264F
- Primary text and symbols: White #FFFFFF
- Divider: #DCE6F5
- Typeface: Montserrat
- SYD: 280 px, ExtraBold (800)
- Ampersand (&): 170 px, Regular (400)
- Billing: 82 px, SemiBold (600)
- Accounting: 68 px, SemiBold (600)
- Preserve the approved hierarchy: SYD is the dominant wordmark; Billing & Accounting sits beneath it.
- Keep proportions and spacing consistent when scaling.

## Android launcher assets
- `icon-only.svg` is the complete master artwork on the navy background.
- `icon-foreground.svg` is the transparent adaptive-icon foreground.
- `icon-background.svg` supplies the Midnight Navy adaptive-icon background.
- The Android build installs Montserrat before running Capacitor Assets so the SVG text is rendered with the approved typeface.

## Project-wide usage
Use this palette, typeface, hierarchy, and proportions for future SYD logos, app icons, and brand treatments. Do not substitute colors or fonts without approval.
